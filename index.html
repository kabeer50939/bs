The Beginning

A Legacy of Vision and Resilience

Dhoria Academy was brought into existence on 22 May 2006 by a young and educated philanthropist group under the leading role of Sir Hassan Ali Moosa. Witnessing the educational challenges faced by local students in English language learning and computer sciences, they set out to establish a forum for their development. Operating with extremely limited financial resources, they bravely launched their mission from a modest shop in a low-valued area of Gwadar, passionately spreading their cause to anyone who would listen.

The group persevered through numerous trials, ups, and downs. Through the tireless efforts of Sir Hassan Ali Moosa, government attention was compelled to recognize Dhoria's noble cause. The academy's first major breakthrough came when Mir Abdul Ghafoor Kalmati generously allotted a piece of land and sponsored most of the proper campus construction. Further rooms were constructed by Sir Majid Sohrabi, and the institute's stabilization was heavily invigorated by Mir Hammal Kalmati and Mir Farooq Kalmati.

Today, Dhoria is a fully-fledged academy housing the Mir Abdul Ghafoor Kalmati Library, serving as an ideal beacon of free educational excellence for the youth of Gwadar.

Jashn-e-Sahil Festival

A premier sports, athletic, and oceanic cultural festival hosted by Dhoria Academy in Gwadar to showcase local talents and foster youth leadership.

Beach Athletics & Marine Sports

Jashn-e-Sahil hosts highly anticipated coastal and marine sports events drawing participants from across Pakistan. The ocean breeze sets the stage for high-energy athletics and community celebrations.

  • Gwadar Beach Marathon: Engaging hundreds of local youth in long-distance racing along the scenic coastline.
  • Traditional Boat Racing: Supporting and showcasing the historical maritime skills of Gwadar's fishing communities.
  • Beach Volleyball & Soccer: Fostering coordination, healthy competition, and sportsmanship on the sands.
Athletics

Folk Traditions & Embroidery Display

Dedicated to preserving and promoting Balochi heritage, Jashn-e-Sahil transforms the coastline into an active museum of local craftsmanship, music, and traditional performances.

  • Traditional Balochi Dance: Highlighting local youth presenting Lewa and Chap dances in traditional attire.
  • Embroidery Exhibitions: Showcasing premium handmade needlework from our Women's Vocational Center.
  • Balochi Poetry Gatherings: Creating a platform for local poets and literary discussions.
Culture

Drama, Debates & Youth Talent Shows

Fostering academic and creative confidence, our students and local youth take center stage in presenting theatrical performances, public speaking matches, and artistic displays.

  • Creative Drama Plays: Educational and socially conscious theatre performed entirely by academy students.
  • Bilingual Debate Forums: Providing structured opportunities for English and Balochi speaking talent.
  • Arts and Craft Show: Exhibiting the best drawings, digital arts, and practical models created in the computer lab.

Our History & Vision

A legacy of over 20 years reshaping modern education.

Our History

Dhoria Academy Gwadar was established on 22 May 2006 by a group of dedicated philanthropists under the leadership of Sir Hassan Ali Moosa, with the vision of providing accessible education and practical skill development in Gwadar. The academy was founded to address the growing need for English language education, computer literacy, and vocational training in the region.

The journey began in a small setup in Gwadar, where limited resources were met with strong determination, commitment, and a shared belief in educational empowerment. Despite early challenges, the academy continued to grow through consistent effort and community trust.

A major turning point came with the valuable support of Meer Abdul Gafoor Kalmati and his sons, who contributed significantly to strengthening and developing the institution. Alongside this, the dedicated teaching staff and faculty played a vital role in maintaining academic quality, while Sir Hassan Ali Moosaâ€™s continuous leadership and vision kept the mission alive and focused.

Over time, Dhoria Academy expanded into a recognized educational center, establishing essential facilities such as a library and learning spaces, and strengthening its role in academic and skill-based education.

Today, the academy stands as a symbol of progress, resilience, and community-driven education, dedicated to shaping skilled, confident, and culturally aware individuals for a better future.

Our Vision

Dhoria Academy Gwadar aims to empower the local community through quality education and practical skills, with a strong focus on English language, computer literacy, and vocational training.

Its vision is to develop confident, skilled, and self-reliant individuals, especially youth and women, while also promoting Balochi culture and literature. The academy strives to create opportunities that prepare students for modern careers and a better future.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, quality education and practical skill development to students, with a focus on English language learning, computer literacy, and vocational training.

The academy is committed to empowering youth and women, promoting Balochi culture and literature, and creating a supportive environment that helps individuals become skilled, confident, and self-reliant members of society.

Geographic Boundaries

Dhoria Academy is situated at the Gwadar Fish Harbor Road near the famous Baba-e-Bezinjo Football Stadium. You can easily navigate to us using these local reference points:

  • North Boundary: Mullaband Girls High School Gwadar
  • South Boundary: Main Gwadar Deep Sea Port Road
  • East Boundary: Gold Star Fish Factory
  • West Boundary: Historic Lighthouse Tower

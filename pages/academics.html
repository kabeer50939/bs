Our Curriculum

Dhoria Academy Gwadar offers a practical and skill-based curriculum focused on meeting the educational needs of the local community. The academy is widely recognized for its strong emphasis on English language learning and computer education, helping students develop essential modern skills.

In addition to academic learning, the curriculum includes professional and skill development programs, particularly for youth and women. These programs focus on English language courses, computer skills training, and embroidery, enabling students to become independent and career-ready.

The academy also promotes a culture of learning through its library facilities, encouraging reading, research, and intellectual growth. By combining language education, digital skills, and creative training, Dhoria Academy aims to build confident, skilled, and socially responsible individuals.

Annual Flagship Festival

Jashn-e-Sahil Festival

Dhoria Academy Gwadar has a proud legacy of organizing the Jashn-e-Sahil (Festival of the Coast) for 5 consecutive years. This prestigious cultural and oceanic athletic festival unites sports enthusiasts and cultural artists from across Gwadar and Makran.

  • Oceanic Races: Traditional Boat & Kati Races at East Bay Gwadar
  • Campus Athletics: Chair racing, tug-of-war, and track sports
  • Cultural Arts: Traditional Lewa dance & Jonz Art stage theater

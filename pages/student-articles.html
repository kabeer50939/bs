Computing & Digital Literacy

Dhoria Computer Center: Empowering Gwadar's Digital Frontier

From its humble inception, the Dhoria Computer Center has been a central pillar of developmental progress in Gwadar, solving a critical gap in digital literacy among local youth through fully sponsored training programs in IT and office automation.
Tech Faculty Established 2006

From its humble inception, the Dhoria Computer Center has been a central pillar of developmental progress in Gwadar. Established to solve a critical gap in digital literacy among local youth, the center has provided fully sponsored training programs covering core systems and automation tools.

Our specialized modules in MS-Windows operations, professional MS-Word formatting, and highly valuable MS-Excel analytical sheets equip students with immediate office readiness. For advance scholars, our Webpage Design programs introduce HTML, CSS, and database fundamentals.

By providing top-notch computer labs completely free of cost, we have empowered hundreds of young men and women across Gwadar—bridging the digital divide and lighting the path of technical literacy.

Heritage & Vocational Craft

Preserving the Golden Threads of Balochi Hand Embroidery

An in-depth exploration of the intricate needlework patterns, maternal heritage, and economic self-reliance fostered through Dhoria Academy's morning vocational shifts for local women of Gwadar and Makran.
Madam Ruqayya Baloch April 2026

Every stitch in traditional Balochi needlework carries centuries of heritage, patient craftsmanship, and regional identity. At Dhoria Academy Gwadar, our dedicated morning vocational sessions provide tuition-free instruction in traditional and modern tailoring, enabling local women to develop valuable vocational skills.

Through community exhibitions and collaborative student projects, these exquisite handicrafts are preserved and promoted, celebrating the cultural ingenuity of Makran's artisans.

Library & Literature

Mir Abdul Ghafoor Kalmati Library: Fostering Reading in Gwadar

Discovering how the Mir Abdul Ghafoor Kalmati Library became Gwadar's sanctuary for intellectual curiosity, competitive exam preparation, and research publications for students of all levels.
Academy Librarian May 2026

Named in honor of community visionary Mir Abdul Ghafoor Kalmati, the academy library houses reference texts, historical archives, and competitive examination study guides. Open during both morning and evening shifts, the library serves students and researchers alike.

Through open-door study hours, digital catalogs, and quiet reading areas, the library continues to cultivate analytical thinkers and lifelong learners across Gwadar.
